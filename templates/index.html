Precision
Meaning: Accuracy of positive predictions. Out of all positive detections the model made, it represents the percentage that were actually correct. Higher precision means fewer false alarms.
Formula: TP / (TP + FP)

Recall (Sensitivity)
Meaning: Detection coverage. Out of all actual positive objects present in the images, it represents the percentage that the model successfully found. Higher recall means fewer missed objects.
Formula: TP / (TP + FN)
